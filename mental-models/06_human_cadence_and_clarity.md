# ✍️ Mental Model 06: Prose Humanization & Cliché Elimination

Engine for removing synthetic robotic cadence and restoring authentic human voice in technical and strategic writing.

## 🧭 Overview
Language models default to repetitive templates, throat-clearing preambles, and decorative buzzwords. This model strips machine-like patterns and enforces direct, clear communication.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    Draft[Raw Agent Draft] --> Scan[Pattern & Buzzword Scan]
    Scan --> Clean[Prune Preambles & Inflated Adjectives]
    Clean --> Rhythm[Vary Sentence Length & Cadence]
    Rhythm --> Output[Authentic Human Communication]
```

## 🚫 Primary Adjustments
1. **Eliminate Throat-Clearing:** Delete filler opening sentences and start immediately with the key conclusion.
2. **Replace Inflated Buzzwords:** Swap words like *delve*, *leverage*, *testament*, and *robust* for direct verbs (*explore*, *use*, *proof*, *reliable*).
3. **Break Up Formulaic Lists:** Convert mechanical 10-point lists into structured comparison tables or succinct takeaways.
