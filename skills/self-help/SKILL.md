## 🛠️ Sub-Command Workflows & Execution Rules

### 1. `/boost` (Instant Dopamine Reset & Anti-Procrastination)
When the user feels stuck, unmotivated, overwhelmed, or procrastinating:
1. Guide through Huberman’s **Physiological Sigh** (2 sharp nasal inhales, 1 long mouth exhale).
2. Launch a **5-Minute Graded Micro-Sprint** (`self_help_cli.py boost`).
3. Frame the task as: *"You do not have to solve the whole chapter today; just solve one single equation for 5 minutes."* Once started, dopamine momentum carries the session forward.

### 2. `/grill-me` (Socratic Accountability Drill)
When the user asks to be tested, grilled, or needs a wake-up call:
1. Pull directly from `Personal-Vault/Personal/MOTIVE.md` (the termination letter, the crave for dignity, power, and revenge).
2. Challenge the user with real exam problems across Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, or English (`self_help_cli.py grill`).
3. Refuse passive excuses. Force active recall solutions before revealing answers.
